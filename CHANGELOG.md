# Changelog

## 0.17.0
- Adventure playtest becomes a full run: pick the Knight or Scout, travel a branching map of fights, elites, shops, rests, events, and forage stops, and face the Old Oak at the end. Winning a run unlocks the Herbalist.
- Beaten enemies drop loot and supplies. Their harvest colors, shown from the start of the fight, level up readied items after the kill.
- Between puzzles, rearrange gear between four slots and a three-item pack, use potions and bombs, or flee a fight. Lingering enemies enrage or run off with their loot.
- Tap an item, the enemy, or your health bar during a puzzle for exact numbers, readiness, and consumables.

## 0.16.0
- Add optional 11×11 and 12×12 puzzles, with new region colors in both themes. New installs start with sizes 5×5 through 10×10 selected.
- Puzzles generate faster, especially larger hard puzzles.

## 0.15.8
- A stump's row, column, or color flashes only the first time it is fully marked, not again after unmarking and remarking an X.

## 0.15.7
- "Perfectly Marked" no longer shows when the board has a red X from a wrong guess.

## 0.15.6
- The adventure puzzle timer now shows the time spent on the current adventure puzzle.

## 0.15.5
- Export adventure data works on Android: the shared file is plain text, and the game downloads it instead if sharing fails.

## 0.15.4
- Undo after keeping What if? changes takes back the kept X's in one step, instead of an earlier move.

## 0.15.3
- iPhone and iPad no longer show the full screen button, because Safari drops out of full screen on any downward swipe. Add Stumped to the Home Screen to play without browser bars.

## 0.15.2
- Export adventure data saves a file: phones and tablets open the share sheet, other browsers download it.

## 0.15.1
- Adventure playtest: the shield blocks more than herbs heal for the same color size, and items that take every color show a color wheel.
- Adventure playtest: choose Wanderer, Coverage, Overlap, Specialist, or Hunter before a new run, with a short description and equipment preview.
- Adventure data records the selected loadout and its exact starting equipment; solve data includes the loadout for comparison.

## 0.15.0
